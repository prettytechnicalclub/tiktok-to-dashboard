# Video takeaways prompt

Use after `video-review` has run. Give Claude the `reviews/` folder (or the `batch-<date>.md`
file) and paste this.

---

These are transcripts, captions and screenshots of videos I saved. Go through all of them and:

1. **Group by theme** (e.g. posture, glutes and core, face, hair, body care, skincare, business,
   content, AI tools, style, mindset). Skip memes, music clips and recipes unless I ask.
2. **Star the overlaps.** When the same exercise, step, product or idea appears in 3 or more
   videos, mark it ⭐ and say how many videos it was in.
3. **Build programs** from the starred moves: a short daily version and a 2–3×/week version,
   with sets/reps or times. Keep the order the creators agree on (e.g. loosen the front of the
   body before strengthening the back; neck before face).
4. **Pick products** when videos list a lot: the 1–2 per step that came up most, with a budget
   and an upgrade option.
5. **Research list:** every time a video points to another video, a book, a tool, a person or
   a study, list it with a link.
6. **Flag** claims that look unproven or risky, and anything that needs a professional.
7. **Note gaps:** videos with no speech (music only) were only seen through the caption and
   screenshots; say which ones.
8. **Propose dashboard updates** as a numbered list (which page, which habit or card, what
   changes). Don't change anything until I say which numbers to do.

Be direct. Use my words for things. No filler.
